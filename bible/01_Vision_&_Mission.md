# Vision

SoundBrain is an AI Audio Engineering Platform.

Its purpose is to evolve from a traditional audio analyzer into an
intelligent engineering system capable of understanding audio, reasoning
like an experienced engineer, learning from previous work, and
eventually performing engineering tasks autonomously.

Ultimate Goal:

Create an AI system capable of assisting---or eventually
replacing---large portions of the technical workflow involved in
professional mixing and mastering.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Mission

Build the most complete AI-powered audio engineering platform with a
modular, scalable, maintainable architecture.

The platform should:

-   Understand music.
-   Understand engineering decisions.
-   Understand references.
-   Explain every recommendation.
-   Learn continuously.
-   Improve over time.

# Success Criteria

A successful SoundBrain should:

☐ Understand audio

☐ Detect problems

☐ Explain problems

☐ Compare references

☐ Suggest engineering solutions

☐ Learn from previous projects

☐ Become smarter after every release

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Design Philosophy

-   Simplicity before complexity.
-   Stability before features.
-   Validation before expansion.
-   Engineering over assumptions.
-   Modular architecture.
-   Runtime-first development.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Non-Goals

SoundBrain is NOT intended to be:

-   Only a loudness analyzer.
-   Only a spectrum analyzer.
-   Only an AI chatbot.
-   Only a plugin recommender.

Its goal is complete engineering intelligence.
